## a16: navjaat bachche ko janam ke samay kaun se teeke lagte hain

### A

Navjaat bachche ko janam ke samay ye teen teeke dete hain:

- BCG ka teeke lagaya jata hai, jo bacche ko TB se bachata hai [5]  
- OPV (oral polio vaccine) dene se polio se suraksha milti hai, isko janam ke pehle 15 dinon mein dena chahiye [4]  
- In teeno ko har jagah, chahe ghar par ya sansthan mein janam hua ho, dena chahiye [1]  
- Agar koi bache ko ye teeke na diye gaye hon, to jald se jald diye jayein [4]  

Doctor ya ASHA se sampark karein agar koi sandeh ho.

Kisi bhi dawai ki sahi matra umar aur wajan par depend karti hai. ASHA didi, PHC doctor ya pharmacist se poochhein.

### B

Navjaat bachche ko janam ke samay kuch teeke lagane chahiye, jo ki bahut zaroori hain [1][2][5].

Here's what the sources say:
*   BCG teeka lagana chahiye, yeh baby ko serious TB illness se bachata hai [5].
*   Hepatitis B ka birth dose baby ko janam ke 24 ghante ke andar dena chahiye, yeh hepatitis B infection se bachata hai [1][5].
*   OPV 0 teeka bhi janam ke andar, 15 din tak dena chahiye, jo baby ko polio se bachata hai [1][4][5].
*   Yeh teeke institutional aur non-institutional deliveries mein, public aur private sectors mein dono jagah lagaye jaate hain [1].
*   Agar baby premature hai ya uska weight kam hai toh bhi immunization schedule start karna chahiye [3].

Agar baby ko allergy hai ya woh seriously ill hai toh teeka lagane se pehle doctor se baat karna chahiye [3]. 
Agar koi problem ho toh ASHA worker, PHC doctor ya 108 par call karein.
