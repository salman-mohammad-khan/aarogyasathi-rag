## Replies

### Garlic can cure dengue fever

[Fact check] MYTH
Garlic has no antiviral effect against dengue virus. There is no cure for dengue - treatment is supportive: rest, fluids and fever management. Believing such remedies delays proper care and can be dangerous.

Based on a curated list of common health myths checked against WHO Dengue Treatment Guidance, NVBDCP, MoHFW.

### Giloy juice cures dengue

[Fact check] PARTLY TRUE
Some preliminary studies suggest giloy may support immunity and platelet recovery, but it is NOT a proven cure for dengue. Dengue has no specific cure - treatment is rest, fluids and medical monitoring. Do not rely on giloy instead of seeing a doctor.

Based on a curated list of common health myths checked against WHO Traditional Medicine, Limited clinical evidence.

### Papaya leaf juice raises platelet count in dengue

[Fact check] PARTLY TRUE
Small studies suggest papaya leaf extract may support platelet recovery in dengue, but it is NOT a cure and is not a standard treatment. The real treatment is hydration, rest and medical monitoring. Never rely on this alone - see a doctor.

Based on a curated list of common health myths checked against WHO Dengue Guidance, NIH studies (small trials).

### ORS should be stopped if the child vomits

[Fact check] MYTH
ORS should not be stopped if the child vomits. According to the guidelines, if the child vomits, the mother should clean the area, wash hands with soap and water, and then administer ORS more slowly [2]. Vomiting does not mean treatment should stop; instead, ORS should continue in a controlled manner to prevent dehydration.

Based on 1 official document(s) in the health library.

### Toothpaste should be applied on a burn

[Fact check] MYTH
Toothpaste, butter, oil, or turmeric should NEVER be applied to burns. They trap heat inside the skin tissue, increase the risk of severe bacterial infection, and complicate clinical care. Correct immediate first aid: cool the burn under clean, running tap water for 15-20 minutes and seek medical care for deep or blistered burns.

Based on a curated list of common health myths checked against WHO Burns Care, MoHFW Burns Guidelines.

### Drinking lemon water every morning burns belly fat

[Fact check] LIKELY MYTH
Drinking lemon water does not specifically burn belly fat. Weight loss requires overall fat reduction through diet and exercise. While lemon water is hydrating, it has no special fat-burning properties. For effective and safe results, consult a healthcare provider.

General view, not from a verified source. Please confirm with a doctor or your ASHA worker.

### Dengue only spreads at night

[Fact check] MYTH
The Aedes aegypti mosquito that spreads dengue bites mainly during the day - especially early morning and before sunset. Protection is needed in daytime too.

Based on a curated list of common health myths checked against WHO Dengue Fact Sheet.

### Dengue spreads through the bite of Aedes mosquitoes

[Fact check] VERIFIED
Dengue virus is transmitted to people through the bites of infected female mosquitoes, primarily Aedes aegypti and to a lesser extent Aedes albopictus. These mosquitoes typically bite during daytime hours, especially early morning and before dusk.

Based on a curated list of common health myths checked against WHO Dengue Factsheet, National Vector Borne Disease Control Programme (NVBDCP).

### ORS helps prevent dehydration in diarrhoea

[Fact check] VERIFIED
Yes. ORS replaces water and salts lost in stools and prevents dangerous dehydration. Give after every loose stool. Continue feeding and breastfeeding.

Based on a curated list of common health myths checked against WHO Diarrhoeal Disease Treatment, NHM, MoHFW.

### TB ka ilaaj poora karna zaroori hai, beech mein dawai nahi chhodni chahiye

[Fact check] SACH (VERIFIED)
Haan, TB ka ilaaj poora karna bahut zaroori hai aur beech mein dawai nahi chhodni chahiye [1]. Agar ilaj adhura chhod diya jaye, to mariz ko zyada takleef ho sakti hai, maut ka khatra badh sakta hai aur drug-resistant TB fail sakta hai [1]. Sahi dose aur sahi samay tak dawai lena hi ilaaj ki safalta ki kunji hai.

Health library ke 1 official document(s) ke aadhar par.

### कुत्ते के काटने पर घाव को साबुन और पानी से धोना चाहिए

[फैक्ट चेक] सत्य
कुत्ते के काटने पर घाव को तुरंत साबुन और पानी से धोना चाहिए। यह घाव से रेबीज वायरस को हटाने में मदद करता है [1][2][3][4]। घाव को कम से कम 15 मिनट तक धोना चाहिए और इस पर मिर्च, तेल, हल्दी आदि नहीं लगाना चाहिए [4]। इसके बाद एंटीसेप्टिक लगाया जा सकता है और चिकित्सा सुविधा में तुरंत परामर्श लेना चाहिए।

### Mosquito nets help prevent malaria

[Fact check] VERIFIED
Mosquito nets, especially insecticide-treated nets (ITNs) and long-lasting insecticidal nets (LLINs), are effective in preventing malaria by acting as a physical barrier and killing or repelling mosquitoes [1]. ASHA workers distribute LLINs and educate communities on their proper use for malaria prevention [2]. Using mosquito nets is a key personal protection measure recommended by WHO [1].

Based on 2 official document(s) in the health library.

### Zinc is given to children with diarrhoea

[Fact check] VERIFIED
The sources confirm that zinc is given to children with diarrhoea. Zinc supplements are recommended for children aged 2 months to 5 years for 14 days, even if diarrhoea stops [1][4][5]. It helps in recovery and is to be given along with ORS.

Based on 3 official document(s) in the health library.
