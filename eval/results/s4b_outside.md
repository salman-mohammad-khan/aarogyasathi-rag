## Answers

### How can I stop hair fall? (library)

You can reduce hair fall with simple care steps.

• Wash your hair regularly with mild soap or shampoo—every 2 to 3 days in summer and once a week in winter [1].  
• Oil your scalp and massage gently to strengthen hair roots [1].  
• Use a wide-toothed comb and avoid tight hairstyles to prevent breakage [1].  
• Clean your comb regularly and do not share it with others [1].  
• If you have dandruff or lice, use anti-dandruff or anti-lice shampoo and keep clothes and bedding clean [1].  

See a doctor at the nearest AAM-SHC or PHC if hair fall is severe or linked with illness like diabetes [1].

### chehre ke pimple hatane ka gharelu upay batao (trusted_web)

Chehre ke pimple kam karne ke liye kuch asaan gharelu upay hai:

- Din mein ek ya do baar halki saaf soap se chehre ko dhoye, khas kar exercise ke baad [1][2].  
- Baal baar baar na chhuye aur chehre se door rakhe [3].  
- Teli wale cosmetics ya creams se bachein [3].  
- Chehre par make up raat bhar na chhode [3].  
- Acne ke liye benzoyl peroxide ya salicylic acid wala wash istemal kar sakte hai [1].

Agar pimple dard deta hai, badh raha hai ya zyada pareshan kar raha hai, toh doctor ya ASHA worker se milna chahiye.

Links: https://medlineplus.gov/ency/patientinstructions/000750.htm

### How to reduce dandruff? (library)

To reduce dandruff, follow these simple steps:

- Wash hair regularly using soap or an anti-dandruff shampoo [2].  
- Keep the scalp clean and avoid oil buildup, which can worsen dandruff [5].  
- Brush the scalp gently to improve circulation and remove flakes [5].  
- Avoid sharing combs or brushes to prevent infection [2].  
- Clean the comb regularly to reduce germs and skin flakes [2].  

If dandruff persists or worsens, see a doctor or call 108 for advice.

Links: https://www.nhsrcindia.org/sites/default/files/2026-10/Integrated%20ASHA%20Module%20Part%20B.pdf, https://www.nhsrcindia.org/sites/default/files/2026-10/Integrated%20ASHA%20Module%20Part%20C_0.pdf

### कंप्यूटर पर काम करने से आंखों में जलन होती है, क्या करें? (library)

कंप्यूटर पर काम करने से आंखों में जलन हो सकती है। इससे बचने के लिए:

• हर 20 मिनट में 20 सेकंड के लिए 20 फीट दूर कुछ देखें (20-20-20 नियम)[1][3]।  
• आंखों को आराम दें और मोबाइल, कंप्यूटर या टीवी का कम समय तक उपयोग करें[1][3]।  
• विटामिन ए युक्त भोजन जैसे गाजर, पालक और अंडा खाएं[1][3]।  
• आंखों को न रगड़ें और साफ पानी से आंखों को धोएं अगर जलन हो[2]।  
• अगर लालिमा, पानी या दृष्टि कम हो, तो तुरंत नजदीकी आरोग्य श्री केंद्र (AAM-SHC) जाएं[3]।  

डॉक्टर से सलाह लें या 108 पर कॉल करें।

Links: https://www.nhsrcindia.org/sites/default/files/2026-10/Integrated%20ASHA%20Module%20Part%20C_0.pdf

### raat ko neend nahi aati, kya karu (library)

Raat ko neend nahi aane ki samasya hai to kuch asaan upay apnaye:

- Niyamit samay par sone aur uthne ki aadat dalen [1].  
- Dopahar mein lambi siesta na karen [1].  
- Shaam ko 5 baje ke baad chai, coffee na piye [1].

### Is it good to drink warm water in the morning? (trusted_web)

Yes, drinking water in the morning is good for health [1].  

- Warm water can help with hydration after a night’s sleep [1].  
- It may support digestion and help prevent constipation [1].  
- Adding a slice of lemon can improve taste and encourage more water intake [2].  
- Water has no calories, so it is a healthy choice instead of sugary drinks [1].  
- Your body needs water daily, and morning is a good time to start [1].  

See a doctor if you have ongoing stomach issues or trouble staying hydrated.

Links: https://www.cdc.gov/healthy-weight-growth/water-healthy-drinks/index.html

### mouth ulcer ho gaya hai, kya kare (library)

Mouth ulcer ho gaya hai to ye kare:

• Garm namak wale paani se kuch baar din me kulu kare [1][2]  
• Khane me tez masale, khatte ya garam chiz se bachen [2]  
• Tobacco, gutkha, khaini, supari kuch mat lagaye aur use band kare [2][3]  
• Daaton ko saaf rakhe, do baar brash kare aur khane ke baad kulu kare [2]  
• Agar ulcer 2 hafte se zyada ho gaya hai ya dard, soojh, ya muh kholne me takleed ho to turant CHC ya PHC le jaye [1][4]  

Doctor ko dikhana jaroori hai agar ulcer jaldi na bhaje.

### How much water should I drink in a day? (library)

You should drink plenty of water every day to stay healthy.

- Drink 8 to 12 glasses of water daily, depending on your needs [1][2].  
- If you are breastfeeding, aim for 10–12 glasses of water each day [1].  
- During hot weather or physical activity, drink even more to avoid dehydration [4].  
- Include fluids like buttermilk, lassi, coconut water, and fresh fruit juices if you can [2][4].  
- Do not wait until you feel thirsty to drink — keep sipping water throughout the day [4].  

See a doctor or call 108 if you feel dizzy, very tired, or have dark urine, as these may be signs of dehydration.

### kamar dard ke liye exercise batao (library)

Kamar dard ke liye kuch aise exercise acchhe hote hain jo halki chalne, jor na daale aur dard badhaye bina jor de.  

- Halka chalna rozana 30-45 minute tak kam se kam 4-5 din in hafte ke liye fayde ke deta hai [4].  
- Yoga ke kuch asan jaise kamar ke liye halka stretch, balance aur flexibility ke liye acchhe hote hain [2][3].  
- Passive ya active joint movement agar patient chal na pa raha ho, toh prashikshit dekhbhal wala kar sake, lekin dard ya soojan ho toh na kare [1].  
- Pelvic floor exercise bhi kamar ke dabav kam kar sakte hain, khas kar agar peshab rokne me dikkat ho [5].  
- Agar tez dard, soojan ya chot lagne ka shak ho, toh exercise na kare [1].  

Doctor ya CHO se salah le agar dard jyada ho ya kam na ho.

### pregnancy mein baal jhadna normal hai kya (library)

Pregnancy mein baal jhadna normal hai kya

- Haan, pregnancy ke dauran baal girna aam baat hai aur zyadatar mahilo mein dekha jata hai [3].  
- Yeh hormones ke chakkar mein ho sakta hai, jaise estrogen aur progesterone ke badlav ki wajah se [3].  
- Baal girna aksar delivery ke baad bhi jari rahta hai, lekin thoda samay baad wapas badhne lagte hain [3].  
- Sahi poshak ahar jaise hari patte wali sabzi, daal, doodh, dahi, paneer, anda khana zaroori hai taki baal aur sharir dono ko sahara mile [3].  
- Agar bahut jyada baal gir rahe hain ya ek saath jhad rahe hain, to PHC doctor ya ASHA didi se baat karein [3].

Dhyan rakhein ki koi bhi chij jaise weight kam hona, kamzori, ya dant dard bhi ho to turant doctor ko dikhayein.

### मेरे बच्चे के बाल बहुत झड़ रहे हैं (library)

आपके बच्चे के बाल झड़ना चिंता का विषय हो सकता है, लेकिन कुछ सावधानियां बरतकर इसे कम किया जा सकता है।

• बालों को नियमित रूप से तेल लगाकर मालिश करें और हल्के हाथ से धोएं [5]।  
• बालों को ढीला बांधें, तनाव न डालें ताकि वे टूटे नहीं [5]।  
• साफ कंघी का उपयोग करें और दूसरों के साथ कंघी साझा न करें [5]।  
• अगर बाल टूट-टूटकर झड़ रहे हैं या पतले हो रहे हैं, तो विशेष देखभाल की आवश्यकता हो सकती है [5]।  

अगर समस्या बनी रहे, तो बच्चे को आंगनवाड़ी या प्राथमिक स्वास्थ्य केंद्र (PHC) ले जाएं।

### I feel sad and hopeless every day (library)

You are not alone in feeling this way. Here are some steps to help:

- Feeling sad and hopeless nearly every day for two weeks or more can be a sign of depression [2].  
- Use the screening tool: if your total score is more than 3 on the depression checklist, you should be referred [1].  
- Talk to someone you trust, like a family member or your ASHA worker [3].  
- Avoid staying alone; family support is very important [3].  
- Speak to the ASHA worker or go to the PHC to see the MO or CHO for help [1].  

Call 108 or go to the nearest health centre if you feel unsafe or very low.

### Is a lump in the neck dangerous? (library)

A lump in the neck can be a sign of something serious and should not be ignored.

• A lump or swelling in the neck can be a symptom of oral cancer [1].
• It may also be related to infections, thyroid problems, or other health issues.
• In some cases, it could be linked to cancers like breast cancer if the disease has spread [2].
• Risk factors like tobacco and alcohol use may increase the chance of such lumps [4].
• Any persistent lump in the neck should be checked by a health worker or doctor.

See a doctor or call 108 if the lump grows, is hard, or does not go away [1][5].

### PCOS ki dawai kaunsi hai (library)

PCOS ke liye koi ek dawai nahi hai, ye ilaj doctor ke upar nirbhar karta hai [5].  
- Doctor blood sugar, weight, aur dusre bimari jaise BP, diabetes ya kidney ki samasya dekhte hain [5].  
- PCOS ke ilaj mein alag-alag dawaiyan use hoti hain, jaise diabetes ke liye jo dawaiyan chalti hain, wo bhi kabhi-kabhi di jati hain [5].  
- Dawai ka chunav doctor karta hai, jo har vyakti ke hisab se alag ho sakta hai [5].  
- ASHA ya PHC ke doctor se baat karein taaki sahi dawai aur matra pata chale.  
Doctor se milna jaruri hai, khaas kar agar pet dard, irregular periods ya weight badhne ki samasya ho.
